# BellyDSP 0.2.0

- **Eight amps, one at a time.** Five new built-in amps join Glass, Ember and Monolith: Lantern (power-amp breakup), Basalt (fat high gain), Comet (a lead with the mids forward), Forge (tight modern high gain) and Quartz (a scooped, bright lead), each with a head of its own. Pick one from the row of small heads at the top of the Amp page; every amp remembers its own knobs. Program change 1 to 8 on a MIDI footswitch picks them, and your own `.nam` capture is the ninth. Only the amp you hear runs now, so a switch fades in about a tenth of a second later and BellyDSP uses far less CPU. Your presets and sessions carry over by themselves.
- **A closer Match tone.** After you play along, it compares your notes with the recording's one by one, tries pedals and a compressor in front of the amp, and adds a fine correction after the cab: the Match curve on the Cab page, which you can turn up or down. With Separate the guitar first on, Clean up with my take can use your notes to clean the rest of the band out of the recording. Save take keeps a section, its stem and your take together.
- **Lighter on slower computers.** The amp models run about twice as fast. For older machines there's a CPU saver switch on the Output page (off unless you turn it on): it idles the drives you aren't using, runs them at lower oversampling, and moves the Gain in its five steps, for a third to a half less CPU.
- **Fixed:** a preset or session made on a Mac keeps its built-in amps and cab IRs on Windows, and the other way round.

**Windows:** this version passed every test on the build server, but nobody has played it through an ASIO interface on a Windows PC yet. If anything misbehaves with yours, let me know.

Set your audio interface to 48 kHz and its input gain to minimum. If an update ever sounds wrong to you, the previous version is on the releases page.
