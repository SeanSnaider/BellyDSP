# BellyDSP 0.3.0

- **A new look.** BellyDSP is redesigned around playing. The preset and its scene sit big in the middle of the top bar, the amp you're playing stands on a stage with the shelf of eight amps above it, and the scenes run along the bottom of the window. Click any block and its controls open in the panel at the right.
- **Amps and pedals that look like gear.** Every amp has a head of its own, and every effect is a pedal with its own footswitch and LED. A pedal that's off is dimmed, so you can see what's on at a glance.
- **The board in three tabs.** Pre FX, Amp & Cab and Post FX. Each tab has a row of small LEDs, one per pedal, so you can see what's on in a tab you aren't looking at. Drag a pedal along its row to reorder it, press `[` and `]` to switch tabs, and selecting a block anywhere opens its tab.
- **The tuner, Match tone and Settings open over the main screen** and close with Esc. Settings holds the input level, the output and limiter, the CPU saver, MIDI, the audio device and the version.
- **Keys:** the left and right arrows step through presets, 1 to 8 pick a scene, T opens the tuner, and Cmd Z undoes (Ctrl Z on Windows).
- **A new app icon,** in the same orange as the rest of the app.

The sound is unchanged: the amps, cabs and effects are the same as in 0.2.0, and your presets, scenes and sessions open as they were.

**Windows:** this version passed every test on the build server, but nobody has played it through an ASIO interface on a Windows PC yet. If anything misbehaves with yours, let me know.

Set your audio interface to 48 kHz and its input gain to minimum. If an update ever sounds wrong to you, the previous version is on the releases page.
