# BellyDSP 0.1.0

The first public version: a free, open-source guitar amp sim and multi-effects app for macOS and Windows.

- **Three amps, always running.** Glass (clean), Ember (crunch) and Monolith (high gain) switch instantly and click-free, from the panel or a MIDI footswitch. Each amp's Gain knob changes how hard it distorts, not how loud it is. Load your own amp-only `.nam` captures into any slot.
- **A cab with mics you can move.** Two close mics and a room mic, 21 built-in cabinet IRs (public domain), or your own IRs and cab packs.
- **Pedals and effects.** Noise gates (with Learn), compressors, boost and overdrive pedals, EQs, harmonizer, multivoicer, phaser, flanger, bitcrusher, chorus, delay and reverb, in an order you can rearrange.
- **Match tone.** Load a song, pick a section where the lead guitar stands out, and BellyDSP sets its amp, cab and EQ to get close to that recording's tone. Compare the song, the match and your current sound side by side before you apply it.
- **The rest.** A tuner, presets with eight scenes each, undo and redo, and an output limiter so nothing clips.
- **Windows:** ASIO support. Choose your interface's ASIO driver in Options for the lowest latency.
- **Updates install themselves** from now on. BellyDSP checks once a day; on a Mac it installs when you quit, on Windows it asks first.

Set your audio interface to 48 kHz and its input gain to minimum. The download page has the first-launch steps.
