BellyDSP for Windows
===================

1. Windows may warn that the installer is from an "unknown publisher" (SmartScreen: "Windows
   protected your PC"). That's because it isn't signed with a paid certificate. Click "More info",
   then "Run anyway".

2. Set your audio interface to 48 kHz: BellyDSP runs at 48 kHz only and stays silent, with a
   message, at any other rate. Use your interface's own control panel, or Windows Sound settings >
   your device > Properties > Advanced.

3. In BellyDSP, click Options at the top of the window > Audio/MIDI Settings:
     - Audio device type: "Windows Audio (Exclusive Mode)" gives the lowest latency through WASAPI;
       "Windows Audio" (shared) works with everything but adds delay. (ASIO appears only in builds
       made with ASIO support.)
     - pick your interface for output and input, sample rate 48000;
     - turn on only the input your guitar is plugged into (usually Input 1);
     - buffer size: as small as plays without crackles (try 128 samples, then 256);
     - untick "Mute audio input". Until you do, BellyDSP hears silence.

4. Captures and IRs: put .nam captures in %APPDATA%\BellyDSP\models and cab IRs in
   %APPDATA%\BellyDSP\irs (paste the path into File Explorer's address bar). Click an amp's grille to
   load a capture.

Updates: BellyDSP checks once a day. When there's a new version it asks; click "Install update" and
it closes, updates itself, and opens again.
