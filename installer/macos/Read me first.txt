BellyDSP, VERSION_PLACEHOLDER
================================

1. Install: drag "BellyDSP" onto the Applications folder next to it.

2. First launch: macOS will say it can't check BellyDSP for malicious software (it's free software
   from an independent developer, not notarized by Apple). That's expected. To allow it, once:
     - Open BellyDSP from Applications. When the warning appears, click Done (not Move to Trash).
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about "BellyDSP",
       and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
     - Open BellyDSP again and click Open.
   Or, in Terminal, one line instead of all that:
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/BellyDSP.app"

3. Allow the microphone when BellyDSP asks: that's how it hears your guitar through the audio
   interface. (Change it later in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone.)

4. Set your audio interface to 48 kHz: open Audio MIDI Setup (in Applications > Utilities), pick
   your interface, and set Format to 48,000 Hz. BellyDSP runs at 48 kHz only and stays silent, with
   a message, at any other rate.

5. In BellyDSP, click Options at the top of the window > Audio/MIDI Settings:
     - pick your interface for both output and input, sample rate 48000;
     - turn on only the input your guitar is plugged into (usually Input 1);
     - buffer size 128 samples (lower if it stays clean, higher if you hear crackles);
     - untick "Mute audio input". Until you do, BellyDSP hears silence (a yellow bar says so).

6. Captures and IRs: put your .nam captures in ~/Library/Application Support/BellyDSP/models and your
   cab IRs in ~/Library/Application Support/BellyDSP/irs (in Finder: Go > Go to Folder..., paste the
   path). Click an amp's grille to load a capture.

Updates install themselves: BellyDSP checks once a day, downloads a new version in the background,
and installs it the next time you quit. Click "BellyDSP" at the top-left to see your version or check now.
